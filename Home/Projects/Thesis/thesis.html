Characterization of Interference Pattern on Liquid Sheet to Produce Dynamically Controlled Traveling Plasma Grating for Relativistic SHHG

Phase 1

Theory and Fringe Analysis

Observe fringe pattern using diode laser and charaterize the pattern for fringe spacing and other parameters.

Phase 2

Integrate with Astrella & Analyze Fringes

Route Astrella beam through interferometer setup and observe the fringe pattern formed. Place liquid sheet target at interference plane and optimize to achieve efficient fringe visibility.

Phase 3

2 Color Interference

Introduce BBO in path of one of the interfering beams. Optimize interferometer to observe transient traveling grating.
